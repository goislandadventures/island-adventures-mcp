# Island Adventures Earns SBA Veteran-Owned Small Business Certification

There's a little more meaning behind the veteran-owned name on our boats.

Island Adventures, the Islamorada private charter company operated by Candor Multimedia, LLC, has earned certification through the U.S. Small Business Administration's Veteran Small Business Certification (VetCert) program.

For Captain Jim, a U.S. Air Force veteran, the recognition is personal. Island Adventures was built around a straightforward idea: give guests their own boat, their own pace, and a real Florida Keys experience without a crowded tour boat or rigid group itinerary.

Today, that approach shapes every private snorkeling charter, Islamorada Sandbar trip, sunset cruise, and custom day on the water. Our captains work with your group and the actual conditions, whether that means reef snorkeling when the weather cooperates or calmer bayside options when it doesn't.

The SBA VetCert designation independently confirms that Candor Multimedia, LLC meets the program's veteran ownership and control requirements. It is a federal contracting certification, not an endorsement by the SBA of our charters.

**What changes for guests?** Nothing about the private, personal experience you came for. This milestone simply gives us another way to stand behind the veteran-owned business we've built here in Islamorada.

If you're planning a Florida Keys boat day, we'd love to show you around on your own terms.
